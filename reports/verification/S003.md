## Summary

18 claims checked: 18 agree, 0 disagree. Every claim is spoken by the guest, every `t_start` and episode is right, every type and stance holds, and the two measure values (TW-46 10 years, TW-55 6 months) match the transcript. The one `-` claim (TW-15) really contradicts ATS-4, and the four `F!` claims (TW-06, TW-30, TW-38, TW-59) all report a rejection or a screen-out. Fourteen agreements carry nits (TW-06, TW-11, TW-15, TW-16, TW-26, TW-30, TW-38, TW-42, TW-46, TW-48, TW-58, TW-59, TW-60, TW-61); four need no change (TW-04, TW-28, TW-43, TW-55). Of the rules that only TW supports, SKL-7, CHN-8, REG-7, STR-9, STR-10, GAP-3 and SCR-9 are worded within what TW said; ATS-5 drops TW's "usually".

Patterns worth adding to the extraction guide:

- **Everything in `claim_en` must be in an anchor (again the most common nit).** Eight claims take a phrase from the sentence just before or just after the quote: TW-16, TW-26, TW-30, TW-38, TW-58, TW-59, TW-60, TW-61. The 25-word windows make this easy, because the end of an assertion often sits in the next window. Before validating, read the whole window of one sentence on each side of the quote and extend the quote (up to three sentences) to cover every phrase `claim_en` uses.
- **Hedges still drop.** "I don't think there is a problem" (TW-11), "it's probably" (TW-42), "I think it flagged" (TW-60) and "usually" turned into "every" (TW-61). When a claim creates a rule, the dropped hedge passes into the rule text (ATS-5).
- **Name whose rejection an `F!` claim reports.** In TW-38 the straight no is the managers' reaction, while TW fixes formatting problems with the candidate before rejecting. `claim_en` for `F!` should say who rejects (the recruiter, managers, some managers, the employer's filter); TW-30 and TW-59 do this well.
- **The quote must start in the anchored sentence.** TW-42's quote starts at the beginning of s016 while the anchor names s015. `rrs ledger validate` accepts it because it searches the three sentence window; a validator warning when the quote does not start in the named sentence would catch this. The `t_start` is still right here only because S003 has turn-level timing.
- **Measure claims carry the quantity only.** TW-46 adds "highlight the first two experiences" to a STR-10 value; a second assertion belongs in its own claim (here it already exists as TW-44).
- **A rule without a scope puts one recruiter on both sides.** ATS-4 does not say whose screening it describes. TW supports it for the recruiter's own agency (TW-14, `+`) and contradicts it for employer ATSs that use AI (TW-15, `-`). Both mappings are correct, but the tally will show TW for and against the same rule. Consider scoping the rule text (for example "In recruiters' own screening, decisions are made by people reading the CV") or recording the scope in the evidence cell, so that a contradiction comes from a different scope only when the reader can see that.
- **Compound rules again get support from one part.** TW-58 supports the referral and networking half of CHN-1, not "they get the CV read more carefully"; TW-60 supports REG-4 only through an ATS flag. Same pattern as REG-1, REG-4 and CHN-2 in S002.
