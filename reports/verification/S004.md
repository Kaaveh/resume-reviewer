## Summary

11 claims checked: 8 agree, 3 disagree (JB-07, JB-09, JB-38). Every claim is spoken by the guest, every stance is right, and no measure claim was in the set.

Patterns worth adding to the extraction guide:

- **Scope qualifiers cut off at a window boundary.** In JB-07 and JB-09 the qualifier that limits the claim ("on that first phone screening", "I can only speak from my own experience") sits in the sentence just before the quote starts, and `claim_en` loses it. The guide could add to the self-check: read the window before the anchor for scope words (own experience, this client, this stage) and carry them into `claim_en`.
- **Rules worded with a CV conclusion the speaker did not draw.** SCR-5 joins a stated fact (the recruiter must justify each candidate) with an inferred CV rule. When a new rule is worded from a claim, each clause should be stated by that recruiter; otherwise the fact and the CV advice should be separate rules.
- **F! for mechanisms about other screeners.** "Gives people a reason to click past" was typed F! although nobody is reported rejecting. The guide could say that a risk or incentive statement ("gives a reason to", "can count against") is F, and F! needs a reported or categorical rejection.
