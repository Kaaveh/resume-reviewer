S001's NotebookLM speaker labels are unreliable, so every anchor was also read for content: the host's questions sometimes sit inside JA-labelled turns (t0019.s005, t0029.s002, t0041.s001, t0065.s006). ## Summary

47 claims checked: 45 agree, 2 disagree (JA-12, JA-54). Every anchor is in a JA-labelled turn, starts in its anchored sentence and lies within the three sentence window; every `t_start` is null as it should be; every episode is the first anchor's; the four measure values (JA-10 5 to 10 s, JA-32 1 page, JA-74 at most 1 line, JA-81 10 to 30 s) match the transcript. All 12 `F!` claims report a rejection by the recruiter, and the six `-` claims (JA-13, JA-40, JA-46, JA-56, JA-73, JA-104; JA-46 could also be `~`) really contradict their rules. No CV owner's name appears in any checked quote, `claim_en` or `quote_en`, though many anchored sentences contain names right next to the quote (t0001.s081, s087, t0009.s004, t0021.s008, t0093.s003, t0101.s092, s121), so any quote extension must be checked. Twenty-five agreements carry nits; twenty need no change.

Disagreements:
- **JA-12** (STR-11): "کور استرنج" is "core strengths", not a course list; STR-11 rests only on this claim and is worded from the mistranslation.
- **JA-54** (RED-2): missing most of the listed skills (6 of 10) is not "missing a core skill"; needs its own rule.

Rules that only JA supports and that go beyond what JA said: STR-11 (see JA-12), CHN-12 ("only when undecided"; JA glances at cover letters anyway and "maybe" looks when undecided), SCR-15 ("at least half an hour"; JA said half an hour). The other JA-only rules met here (SCR-14, SKL-8, SKL-10, SKL-12, SKL-15, SKL-17, SKL-18, STR-12, STR-15, STR-16, STR-22, REG-13, SEN-7, ACH-6, ACH-11) are worded within JA's words.

Patterns worth adding to the extraction guide:

- **Host words inside guest-labelled turns.** In S001 the host's questions often sit inside JA-labelled turns (t0019.s005, t0029.s002, t0041.s001, t0065.s006), and one anchor quotes the host (JA-48, t0041.s001, a question glossed as a statement). For sources with unreliable labels, read each anchored sentence for a question or a "so you mean" turn; when the guest's answer is in the next turn, anchor there.
- **Gloss every anchor.** Eight checked claims have fewer `quote_en` segments than anchors (JA-05 2 of 6, JA-09 4 of 5, JA-13 1 of 2, JA-30 2 of 5, JA-38 3 of 11, JA-55 1 of 4, JA-56 1 of 2, JA-78 2 of 3). A validator warning when the number of " / " segments differs from the number of anchors would catch it.
- **Transliterated English in Persian ASR.** English terms come through as sound-alike Persian ("کور استرنج" core strengths, "ایبلیوس" AWS, "نوید" novice, "کفارت" for "کفایت", "آردی" already, "راجع به" probably Azure). Read the next sentences before glossing, and say "uncertain reading" in `notes` when a rule depends on the gloss (JA-12 built a rule on a wrong one).
