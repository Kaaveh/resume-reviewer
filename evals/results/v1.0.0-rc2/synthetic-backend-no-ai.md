# CV review: Senior Backend Engineer (Java) for UK product companies, relocating from India

**Verdict:** Borderline

**10-second verdict:** The top of page one shows "Senior Backend Engineer", Java and Spring Boot, and a current senior role at Quillstone Software since Apr 2021. A screener checking for the Java stack keeps reading (SCR-1, SCR-7). The employers are not names a UK screener will recognise (SCR-6), and the first role reads as a list of duties with no result, no scale and no AI use, so a hiring manager who must justify a relocation hire (SEN-6, SCR-15) is likely to stay undecided.

**Target and weighting:** Senior Backend Engineer (Java) at UK product companies, candidate based in India and relocating; from the request, not the default target. No job description was given, so hard requirements are judged against a typical Java backend stack. Match: JA high (UK hiring manager at a tech product company), TW medium-high (UK agency, tech and product), JB medium-high (cross-border hires into the UK and EMEA), SB low-medium (JavaScript and TypeScript startups only). Single source JA rules are raised one level where the CV is in the situation JA described.

## Findings

### High
- **ACH-1** (Consensus 4, High): every role lists duties and technologies, not problem, personal contribution and result: "Responsible for developing REST APIs in Java and Spring Boot", "Worked on microservices", "Handled production support tickets". Fix: rewrite each recent bullet as what was built, what the candidate personally did, and what changed.
- **ACH-6** (Consensus 3, High): no bullet carries a number anywhere on the CV, for example "Worked with Kafka and Redis." Fix: add real figures (latency, throughput, users, error rates, time saved) to at least the top bullets of the two most recent roles. Use only figures the candidate can defend (RED-3).
- **SKL-4** (Supported 2, High): the CV never mentions AI coding tools in the development workflow; the summary, bullets and skills list are silent on it. JA, the high match for UK product companies, reports rejecting on this. Fix: if the candidate uses AI tools in daily work, add one concrete bullet and the tool names to the skills list. Do not add it if it is not true (RED-3). TW makes the same point as a single source (SKL-7).
- **ACH-4** (Consensus 3, High): no side projects or hackathons are listed. Fix: add one or two relevant backend projects with dates and context (STR-15), if the candidate has any.
- **ACH-12** (Single source 1, raised from Medium to High: JA is a high match and reports rejecting on it): the bullets show participation, not ownership: "Participated in code reviews and sprint planning", "Responsible for maintaining the PostgreSQL database". Fix: name work the candidate drove from start to release and followed up afterwards.
- **SKL-10** (Single source 1, raised from Medium to High: JA is a high match and reports rejecting on it): no testing tools appear anywhere on the CV. Fix: name the testing tools actually used (for example the unit and integration test frameworks) and how, in a bullet or the skills list.

### Medium
- **STR-4** (Supported 2, Medium): the summary is generic: "Hard-working team player with strong communication skills." Fix: replace it with what the candidate specialises in and why that fits a UK product company backend role, in 3 to 4 lines (STR-21).
- **SKL-3** (Supported 2, Medium): "Docker" and "MySQL" in the skills list appear in no role, so the reader cannot tell where they were used. Fix: mention them in the role where they were used, or drop them.
- **SEN-5** (Single source 1, raised from Low to Medium: JA is a high match): "10 years of experience" leads a reader to expect senior-level impact, but the senior role shows only routine duties. As a relocation hire the candidate must look stronger than the team's average (SEN-6). Fix: lead the Quillstone role with the biggest result (ACH-10).
- **ACH-7** (Single source 1, raised from Low to Medium: JA is a high match): no scale is given for product, company or team ("Developed backend services in Java"). Fix: add [N users], [N requests per second], [team of N] where true.
- **ACH-13** (Single source 1, raised from Low to Medium: JA is a high match): bullets open with "Responsible for" twice and "Worked on" or "Worked with" twice. Fix: start with varied action verbs (Built, Designed, Migrated, Reduced).
- **SKL-18** (Single source 1, raised from Low to Medium: JA is a high match): the skills list ends with "Git, Jira, Agile", practices every engineer uses. Fix: remove them.
- **SKL-8** (Single source 1, raised from Low to Medium: JA is a high match): the skills section is one flat list. Fix: group it (Languages and frameworks, Data and messaging, Infrastructure, Testing).
- **ACH-9** (Single source 1, raised from Low to Medium: JA is a high match): nothing shows recent learning. Fix: show any new tool, course or project applied in recent work.

### Low
- **REG-5** (Single source 1, Low; SB is a low-medium match): the header says "Pune, India" but not that the candidate would relocate. Fix: add "Open to relocating to the UK" to the header line.
- **REG-15** (Single source 1, Low): right-to-work status for the UK is not stated. Fix: state the actual status in plain words if the candidate holds a UK visa; do not write that sponsorship is needed (REG-2).
- **RED-4** (Single source 1, Low; TW is medium-high, not high, so no raise): "Responsible for developing ..." is the standard phrasing screeners read as generic or AI-written. Fixed by the ACH-1 rewrites.
- **ACH-2** (Single source 1, Low): no role names the business domain or end customers ("Quillstone Software", "Mervale Systems"). Fix: add the domain and who the users were.
- **STR-7** (Single source 1, Low): no company context line under each role; UK screeners will not know these employers (SCR-6). Fix: add 2 to 4 lines on what the company does and the team (STR-8).
- **CHN-5** (Single source 1, Low): no LinkedIn URL in the header. Fix: add it, and keep LinkedIn consistent with the CV (CHN-20).

### Judgment calls
- **SKL-5** (Contested 1-1): no cloud provider is named; the only infrastructure item is "Docker". For: SB says leaders treat clouds as interchangeable and weigh the core stack. Against: JA says cloud providers are not interchangeable. Recommendation: follow JA, the high match. Name the cloud the candidate actually used, in the role where it was used; if none, present deployment work honestly without claiming a provider.
- **REG-16** (Contested 1-1): the CV is in India and the jobs are in the UK. For: TW says location filters and client ATS screen out applicants abroad. Against: JA says skill gaps, not location, cause rejections. Recommendation: fix the High findings first (JA), then state the real city and the relocation line (REG-14, REG-5).
- **REG-17** (Contested 2-1): the +91 number fits the stated city. Recommendation: keep the real number with its country code (REG-3); do not add a number the candidate does not have.
- **STR-2** (Contested range 4): the CV is one page for 10 years. JA prefers one page; TW and JB accept two or more. Recommendation: one page is fine, and the rewrites can take it to two if needed.

## Rewrites

**Summary**
- Before: "Backend engineer with 10 years of experience in Java and Spring Boot. Hard-working team player with strong communication skills."
- After: "Senior backend engineer with 10 years in Java and Spring Boot, building REST APIs and microservices on PostgreSQL, Kafka and Redis. [Domain or product type, for example the kind of platform built at Quillstone]. Recent work: [biggest result with a number]. Based in Pune, open to relocating to the UK."

**Quillstone Software, first bullet**
- Before: "Responsible for developing REST APIs in Java and Spring Boot."
- After: "Built [N] REST APIs in Java and Spring Boot for [product or feature], serving [N users or requests per day]."

**Quillstone Software, database bullet**
- Before: "Responsible for maintaining the PostgreSQL database."
- After: "Owned the PostgreSQL database for [service]: [what was changed, for example indexing or schema migration], cutting [query time or incidents] by [X%]."

**Quillstone Software, microservices bullet**
- Before: "Worked on microservices."
- After: "Designed and shipped [N] microservices for [purpose], from design to production, [result]."

**Quillstone Software, production support bullet**
- Before: "Handled production support tickets."
- After: "Resolved [N] production incidents per [month], and fixed the root cause of [recurring issue], reducing [tickets or downtime] by [X%]."

**Mervale Systems**
- Before: "Worked with Kafka and Redis."
- After: "Built [event pipeline or cache] with Kafka and Redis for [purpose], handling [N messages per second] with [latency or result]."

**Skills**
- Before: "Java, Spring Boot, Hibernate, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Kafka, Redis, Docker, REST, Microservices, Git, Jira, Agile"
- After: "Languages and frameworks: Java, Spring Boot, Hibernate. Data and messaging: PostgreSQL, MySQL, Kafka, Redis. Infrastructure: Docker, [cloud provider used]. Testing: [test tools used]. AI tools: [tools used in daily work]."

## What already works
- The title, Java and Spring Boot are visible at the very top, and the most recent role shows the core stack (SCR-7, SCR-2, RED-2 passes for a Java role).
- Reverse date order with months on every role and no gaps (STR-24, GAP-3).
- Each heading leads with the job title and gives the employer's location (STR-14, STR-19).
- The real city is in the header (REG-14), with email and a phone number with country code (REG-3); no sponsorship statement (REG-2).
- Plain, one-column layout with no photo or graphics (STR-3, REG-7).
- Education names the university and degree (STR-27), placed after experience (STR-26).
- The title is "engineer", not tied to one language (STR-16).

## Beyond the CV
- **CHN-15** (Consensus 3): ask for referrals into UK product companies; a referral gets the CV a closer read, which matters for a relocation candidate.
- **CHN-16** (Supported 2) and **CHN-17** (Supported 2): network alongside applications and send the hiring manager a short message on why the candidate fits.
- **CHN-18** (Consensus 3): keep LinkedIn complete and current, matching the CV's dates and roles (CHN-20), with a standard title (CHN-10).

## Not covered by the evidence
- UK visa routes and sponsorship eligibility are outside the CV rules; general advice: check whether target employers hold a sponsor licence before applying.
- No job description was given, so keyword match (ATS-1, SKL-11) and tailoring (STR-6) could not be checked; rerun the review against a specific ad.
- No recruiter in the evidence specialises in Java backend hiring; JA hires across disciplines at a UK product company.
- General advice: Indian university names may be unfamiliar to UK screeners; the degree line is enough as written.
