## Conventions

- Type: `F` fact or practice, `F!` fact reporting rejection on it, `P` preference or advice.
- Stance: `+` supports the rule, `-` contradicts it, `~` qualifies it (listed, not counted).
- Labels: Consensus (3+ distinct recruiters), Supported (2), Single source (1), Contested (supporters and opposers both exist). Counting rules: ADR 0001.
- Locator: sentence ID, `[HH:MM:SS]` when the source has timing, then the exact quote.
- Only guest claims count; host claims marked independent are listed but not counted.
